# Sing workspace and ImageGen controls — 2026-10-21

## Change

The loaded-song Sing screen now has a stable, compact heading, a full wrapping song title and a separate Edit lyrics action. Optional preparation, studio onboarding and posture guidance are grouped in the keyboard-operable Before you sing disclosure. The lyric sheet scrolls independently above a fixed recording dock. Verse/chorus headings are smaller than lyrics; live word highlighting retains the same font metrics and no longer scales lines. Idle microphone status is Mic off rather than an unmeasured Good rating. Detailed pitch feedback scrolls with the lyrics instead of displacing the recording controls on short phones.

Six transparent raster control assets were created with OpenAI's built-in ImageGen, optimized to 256px and stored in assets/images/sing-*-v1.png. Exact prompts and source filenames are in sing-controls-provenance.json. The icons cover record, pause, resume, stop, marker and metronome. Controls retain native accessible names, visible labels and 44px or larger hit targets. The small transport icons no longer bob continuously. Pause/resume assets preload, and the active record control has a circular shadow boundary.
