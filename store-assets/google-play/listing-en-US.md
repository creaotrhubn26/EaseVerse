# EaseVerse — Google Play listing (en-US)

## App name

EaseVerse

## Short description

Record, practise and review vocals with lyrics and CreatorHub projects.

## Full description

EaseVerse is a focused music workspace for vocalists, songwriters and producers.

Capture vocal ideas and complete takes, organise songs and lyrics, practise difficult sections and review your performance in one calm workflow. Your core recording and session history work locally on your device, without requiring an account.

Sign in with your CreatorHub account when you want to continue a production across CreatorHub Workspace, Sound Room and CreatorHub Pro Tools Companion.

With EaseVerse you can:

- Record vocal sessions and play them back
- Write and organise songs and lyrics
- Practise sections with guided timing and pitch tools
- Review session timing and coaching insights
- Keep local sessions available after restarting the app
- Open the matching CreatorHub Workspace or Sound Room project
- Continue a production handoff from Pro Tools Companion
- Use the core local workflow without creating an account

Microphone access is requested only after you start a recording, live session or voice-analysis feature. Audio is kept on your device by default. Audio that you explicitly choose to analyse, synchronise or share is sent securely to the EaseVerse service.

EaseVerse is part of the CreatorHub music-production workflow.
